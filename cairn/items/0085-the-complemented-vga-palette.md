TempleOS was sixteen colours fixed by covenant. Nether C takes those sixteen
and complements every one.

Fourteen land back inside the palette — blue complements to yellow, green to
light purple, light grey to dark grey. The VGA palette is very nearly its own
opposite.

BROWN (#AA5500) and LTBLUE (#5555FF) have nowhere to go: their complements are
#55AAFF and #AAAA00, which are not VGA colours. Keep both, name them ROT and
BILE, and say so on the site. It is the most honest thing about the project.
