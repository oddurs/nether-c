The nether is unlit, so the lamp starts out. Pressing it swaps to the
original VGA palette on a white ground — the temple's own colours, on the
temple's own ground.

It is the only place in the project where anything ascends, and it is the
same verb as the `nether lamp` rite for the same reason.
