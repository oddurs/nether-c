Two things, and they are the same thing.

## The mark

The project had no emblem, and the noun was sitting in the vocabulary unused: a
**cairn** is a pile of stones somebody stacked by hand to mark a place. It is
already the word this language uses for a name. It is also what you leave at
the mouth of a descent so the next person can find the way.

A temple is built upwards. This is the other thing you can build out of stone.
Drawn in `site/gfx.py` like everything else here, five stones, none of them
symmetric, leaning.

## The position

`ON TERRY DAVIS` is the **last section of the front page**. The last section of
a document is its conclusion, so the page reads: here is Nether C, and what it
is really about is Terry Davis.

The text is fine. The position is not, and it is also unfair to the work: this
project stands up without him now, and leaning on him reads as borrowing.

He gets a page of his own, linked once from the footer, written as a memorial
and not as a justification. Everything else keeps the lineage where lineage
belongs — `spec/90-rationale.md`, and the palette, which is literally derived
from VGA.
